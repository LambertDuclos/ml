# Papers

One folder per paper, `papers/<year>-<short-name>/`, starting with Bengio et al. 2003 at lesson 3.

- `notes.md`, mine, four fixed sections. **Claim**: what the paper says it shows. **Method**: how,
  in my words, with the one equation that matters. **What is new**: against what came before.
  **What is weak**: what I would push back on, with a reason. Read by the critic like lesson notes.
- `repro.py` and `test_repro.py`: reproduce one number or one figure from the paper at a scale the
  M4 can run. The test states the number and the tolerance; the tutor writes it, I write `repro.py`.
- `questions.md`: what I would ask the authors. These also go in the root `questions.md`.
